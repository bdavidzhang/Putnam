\section{Problem}
For nonnegative integers $n$ and $k$, define $Q(n, k)$ to be the coefficient of $x^k$ in the expansion of $(1 + x + x^2 + x^3)^n$. Prove that
\[
Q(n, k) = \sum_{j=0}^n \binom{n}{j} \binom{n}{k - 2j}.
\]

\section{Solution}
We can factor $1+x+x^2+x^3 = \frac{x^4-1}{x-1} = (x+1)(x^2+1)$,
so that the original expression is 
\[P(x) = (x+1)^n(x^2+1)^n = \sum_{i=0}^{n} {n \choose i} x^i \sum_{j=0}^{n} {n \choose j} x^{2j}\] 
then the coefficient for $x^k$
is given by 
\[
Q(n, k) = \sum_{j=0}^n \binom{n}{j} \binom{n}{k - 2j},
\]
where the first term is the coefficient due to $x^2$ and the second due to $x$.
